# Brain Mantra — How to Delete Your Account and Data

This page explains how to delete your Brain Mantra account and everything stored with it.

## Option 1 — Inside the app (fastest)

1. Open Brain Mantra and sign in.
2. On the Home screen, scroll to the bottom and tap the red **Delete Account** button.
3. Read the list of what will be removed, then tap **Delete My Account**.

Your account is deleted immediately.

## Option 2 — You no longer have the app

Email **[SUPPORT_EMAIL]** from the email address linked to your account (or, if you signed in with Google, the Google address you used). Write "Delete my Brain Mantra account" in the subject. We will confirm your identity and complete the deletion within **14 days**.

## What is deleted

- Your account and sign-in (email/password or linked Google account)
- Your display name
- Your persistent score and leaderboard placement
- Your Daily Challenge history

Data stored only on your phone (day streak, last session) is removed when you delete your account in the app or uninstall Brain Mantra.

## What we may keep

Nothing that identifies you is kept after deletion, except where the law requires us to retain a record (for example, a record that a deletion request was processed).

## Automatic deletion

Accounts that stay **inactive for 30 days** are deleted automatically along with their data.
